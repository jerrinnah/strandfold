What we actually do

Support that doesn’t stop at the door.

Getting out is not the end of the story. It is the beginning of a much longer one — the counselling, the court dates, the paperwork, the children’s school fees, the question of where the next month’s income comes from.

Most support ends after the emergency. Ours is built for what follows.

Safe Landing

Rebuild

Why we’re called Strandfold

One strand breaks. Folded, we hold.

A single strand is fragile. Pull it and it breaks. But strands folded together — twisted, layered, held — become rope strong enough to carry weight that would snap any one of them alone.

The older sense of fold is a shelter: an enclosure where the vulnerable are kept through the night. Not forever. Long enough for morning.

That is the work. Not rescue. Not charity. A place to be held, and enough hands folded together to carry what one woman was never meant to carry by herself.
