Stories

From surviving to standing.

These are not case studies. They are accounts of what women built, in their own words, on their own terms.

Every story on this page is shared with the written, informed consent of the woman it belongs to. Names, locations and identifying details have been changed.

Consent can be withdrawn at any time, and any story will be removed within 24 hours of that request.

No photograph on this page depicts a Strandfold participant unless she has specifically asked to be photographed.

How we write these

Dignity over pity.

We start with where she is now, not with what was done to her. We do not trade in tragedy for donations, and no woman is asked to perform her worst day to be worth helping.

Never included

  • Photographs identifying her
  • Her town, if it is small enough to identify her
  • Her employer
  • Her children’s names or schools
  • The abuser’s name
  • Graphic description of violence

Journalists and researchers

We do not connect journalists directly to survivors under any circumstances. We are glad to talk about the work, the data and the policy gaps.
