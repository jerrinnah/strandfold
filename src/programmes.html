Our programmes

Safety is not the finish line.

A woman who is safe but has no income, no counsel and no legal standing has been moved from one kind of trap to another. Our programmes are built as a continuum — most women move through several, at their own pace, over months or years.

Standing Ground

Legal support · For women pursuing protection, separation, custody or prosecution — or simply trying to understand their rights

What it includes

  • Plain-language explanation of your rights under [relevant legislation — e.g. VAPP Act, state family law]
  • Guidance on documenting evidence safely
  • Support obtaining protection orders
  • Court accompaniment, so you never sit in that room alone
  • Referral to pro bono and low-cost legal counsel
  • Support with custody, maintenance and property claims

Important

We provide guidance and accompaniment. We are not a law firm and cannot represent you in court, but we will connect you with someone who can.

Rebuild

Economic independence · For women ready to build an income of their own

What it includes

  • Vocational skills training in [tailoring, catering, hairdressing, digital skills, …]
  • Digital and financial literacy
  • CV writing, interview preparation and job placement support
  • Seed grants and starter equipment for small businesses
  • Twelve months of business mentorship after start-up
  • Savings group membership

Why this exists

Financial dependence is the single most common reason women return to abusive partners. Counselling without income is incomplete care.

Outcome tracked

Percentage of participants earning independent income at 6 and 12 months.
