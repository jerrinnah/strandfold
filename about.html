About us

We stay through the years that follow.

Strandfold is a survivor-led support organisation working at the point where crisis response ends and long-term recovery begins.

The name

Why we’re called Strandfold

A single strand is fragile. Pull it and it breaks. But strands folded together — twisted, layered, held — become rope strong enough to carry weight that would snap any one of them alone.

The older sense of fold is a shelter: an enclosure where the vulnerable are kept through the night, not forever, but long enough for morning.

That is the work. Not rescue. Not charity. A place to be held, and enough hands folded together to carry what one woman was never meant to carry by herself.

Our approach

Built around how trauma actually behaves.

Trauma-informed, always

Our team is trained to recognise that trauma changes how a person remembers, speaks, trusts and decides. A survivor who tells her story out of order is not lying. A survivor who returns to her abuser has not failed.

We build our processes around how trauma actually behaves, not around how we wish it behaved.

Survivor-led, not survivor-managed

Women who have come through our programmes sit on our advisory board and shape our services. The people closest to the problem are closest to the solution.

We do not pressure anyone to leave

This is the hardest thing to explain to donors and the most important thing we do. On average, a woman attempts to leave an abusive relationship several times before it holds.

Each attempt is information, not failure. A woman who knows we will still be here when she returns is a woman who eventually comes back for the last time.

Confidentiality without exception

We do not share names, case files, photographs or identifying details with anyone — media, donors, family, employers — without explicit written consent, which can be withdrawn at any moment.

Core values

Five things we hold to.

She Leads

The survivor decides. We do not decide for her, pressure her to leave, or withdraw support if she returns. We provide options, information and honesty, and we respect the choice she makes with them.

Confidentiality Is Sacred

What is shared with us stays with us. No names, no photographs, no case details without explicit, written, revocable consent.

Dignity Over Pity

We do not trade in tragedy for donations. No shock imagery. No woman is asked to perform her worst day to be worth helping.

Practical Over Performative

Awareness campaigns do not pay rent. Every programme must produce a measurable change in a woman’s safety, health, legal standing or income.

The Long Walk

Recovery is not an event. We measure success in years, not rescues.

What we don’t do

Honesty builds more trust than a long list of claims.

  • We are not an emergency service. In immediate danger, call [emergency number].
  • We do not operate our own shelter [or: our shelter has limited capacity]. We refer to vetted partner facilities.
  • We are not a law firm. We provide legal guidance, documentation support and accompaniment, and connect you with practising counsel.
  • We do not confront, contact or negotiate with abusers.
  • We do not provide financial support for legal fees beyond [scope].

Your safety

Covering your tracks

A browser keeps a record of the pages you visit. If someone else can use your phone or computer, here is how to reduce what they can see.

  1. Use private browsing next time. Chrome: menu → New Incognito tab. Safari: tabs → Private. Firefox: menu → New private window. Pages opened this way are not added to your history.
  2. Clear your history now. Chrome: menu → History → Clear browsing data → choose Browsing history and Cookies. Safari on iPhone: Settings → Safari → Clear History and Website Data.
  3. Delete only some entries if clearing everything would look strange. In the history list you can remove single pages and leave the rest.
  4. Check what else is watching. Location sharing, shared cloud accounts, a linked tablet, or a family plan can show your activity even after you clear history.
  5. Safest of all: use a phone or computer he has never had access to — a friend's, a library's, or one at work.

Quick exit: the red button at the top of every page leaves this site straight away and removes this page from the Back button. You can also press the Esc key twice quickly.

Clearing history is not a guarantee. If you believe your phone is being monitored, contact us from a different device.
