What we actually do

Support that doesn’t stop at the door.

Getting out is not the end of the story. It is the beginning of a much longer one — the counselling, the court dates, the paperwork, the children’s school fees, the question of where the next month’s income comes from.

Most support ends after the emergency. Ours is built for what follows.

See how each programme works

Safe Landing

Immediate crisis response, safety planning, and referral to vetted shelter and medical care.

Inner Work

Trauma-informed counselling, one-on-one and in survivor circles, at no cost.

Standing Ground

Legal guidance, evidence documentation, protection order support and court accompaniment.

Rebuild

Skills training, financial literacy, job placement and small business start-up grants.

Why we’re called Strandfold

One strand breaks. Folded, we hold.

A single strand is fragile. Pull it and it breaks. But strands folded together — twisted, layered, held — become rope strong enough to carry weight that would snap any one of them alone.

The older sense of fold is a shelter: an enclosure where the vulnerable are kept through the night. Not forever. Long enough for morning.

That is the work. Not rescue. Not charity. A place to be held, and enough hands folded together to carry what one woman was never meant to carry by herself.

Your safety

Covering your tracks

A browser keeps a record of the pages you visit. If someone else can use your phone or computer, here is how to reduce what they can see.

  1. Use private browsing next time. Chrome: menu → New Incognito tab. Safari: tabs → Private. Firefox: menu → New private window. Pages opened this way are not added to your history.
  2. Clear your history now. Chrome: menu → History → Clear browsing data → choose Browsing history and Cookies. Safari on iPhone: Settings → Safari → Clear History and Website Data.
  3. Delete only some entries if clearing everything would look strange. In the history list you can remove single pages and leave the rest.
  4. Check what else is watching. Location sharing, shared cloud accounts, a linked tablet, or a family plan can show your activity even after you clear history.
  5. Safest of all: use a phone or computer he has never had access to — a friend's, a library's, or one at work.

Quick exit: the red button at the top of every page leaves this site straight away and removes this page from the Back button. You can also press the Esc key twice quickly.

Clearing history is not a guarantee. If you believe your phone is being monitored, contact us from a different device.
