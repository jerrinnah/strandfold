Volunteer

We need specific skills, and we screen everyone.

Every volunteer completes background screening, safeguarding training and a confidentiality agreement.

No exceptions — including for board members and donors.

Apply to volunteer

We need

  • Licensed counsellors and psychologists
  • Lawyers (family law, criminal law)
  • Vocational trainers
  • Drivers with valid licences
  • Administrative and data support
  • Fundraising and communications

Are you a survivor yourself? Yes, you can volunteer — many of our team are. We ask that you be at a stable point in your own recovery, and we will discuss this openly and supportively during screening.

Your safety

Covering your tracks

A browser keeps a record of the pages you visit. If someone else can use your phone or computer, here is how to reduce what they can see.

  1. Use private browsing next time. Chrome: menu → New Incognito tab. Safari: tabs → Private. Firefox: menu → New private window. Pages opened this way are not added to your history.
  2. Clear your history now. Chrome: menu → History → Clear browsing data → choose Browsing history and Cookies. Safari on iPhone: Settings → Safari → Clear History and Website Data.
  3. Delete only some entries if clearing everything would look strange. In the history list you can remove single pages and leave the rest.
  4. Check what else is watching. Location sharing, shared cloud accounts, a linked tablet, or a family plan can show your activity even after you clear history.
  5. Safest of all: use a phone or computer he has never had access to — a friend's, a library's, or one at work.

Quick exit: the red button at the top of every page leaves this site straight away and removes this page from the Back button. You can also press the Esc key twice quickly.

Clearing history is not a guarantee. If you believe your phone is being monitored, contact us from a different device.
